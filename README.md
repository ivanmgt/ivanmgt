## 🔬 Featured Work
Time-Series Forecasting of ECAL Crystal Calibration with LSTM

Undergraduate thesis — UNAM · CERN/CMS Experiment

Multistep forecasting of radiation damage in PbWO₄ crystals of the Electromagnetic Calorimeter (ECAL) of the CMS detector at the Large Hadron Collider (LHC), CERN.

Designed and benchmarked 9 LSTM architectures in TensorFlow: standard multioutput, bidirectional multioutput, seq2seq (2 variants), and ring-trained versions across full η pseudorapidity rings
Evaluated with MAPE and WMAPE over a 96-step prediction horizon
Best model (seq2seq architecture 1): 40.9% improvement over Naive Baseline
Trained on HPC clusters using SLURM workload manager
Full codebase documented in English
